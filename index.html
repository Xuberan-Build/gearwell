Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this need an MOC? −
No. Our refurbished gearboxes are like-for-like with the original OEM spec: no design changes and no process changes. That's what keeps a replacement out of MOC.
How do you keep rebuilt gearboxes at OEM quality? +
Technicians trained at OEM facilities rebuild every refurbished gearbox. Our testing meets or exceeds OEM standards, including no-load spin testing and hydraulic seal testing.
What's your real emergency response time? +
Call us with a part number and we confirm availability on the call. Orders in before 1 PM ship the same day.
Do you have parts for older Lightnin units? +
Often, yes. We keep limited stock for models going back 30+ years. If Lightnin made it, we may well have the parts.
What if you don't have my part in stock? +
We'll tell you right away and give you a real timeline. No false promises. Our supplier network often finds hard-to-find parts in days, not weeks.
