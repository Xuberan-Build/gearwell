Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you make sure parts meet OEM specs? −
Our parts are sourced to original Lightnin specifications.
What testing do you do before it ships? +
Every part gets a visual inspection. Refurbished gearboxes also get a no-load spin test and performance validation. Full test reports are available on request.
Our unit is down. How fast can you get us parts? +
Stock parts ship the same day on orders in before 1 PM. Custom gearboxes ship within 48 hours. Emergency response is available 24/7.
Can you get obsolete Lightnin parts? +
Yes. We stock parts for equipment going back 30+ years, and our supplier network can often find discontinued components in days, not weeks.
Do you rebuild complete gearboxes? +
Yes. Factory-trained technicians do complete gearbox rebuilds. Every rebuild gets a no-load spin test and performance validation, and comes with full warranty coverage.
What do you need from us to identify the part? +
A part number or the nameplate data. If the part numbers are gone, our technicians can often identify components from photos or drawings.
