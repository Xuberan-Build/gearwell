Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Our unit is down. How fast can you respond? −
Technical staff answer our 24/7 hotline within minutes. On average, we confirm emergency gearbox availability in under 2 hours.
What info do you need from us to match the gearbox? +
The nameplate data, the output speed you need, or your existing part numbers. If you don't have those, our technicians can often work out the spec from photos or drawings.
How do you make sure it matches our existing gearbox? +
Former Lightnin technicians check every spec before the gearbox is configured. Every gearbox gets a no-load spin test before it ships.
What testing do you do before it ships? +
Every refurbished gearbox gets a no-load spin test, a quality check and ratio confirmation. Full test documentation is available on request.
What happens if you don't have the right configuration in stock? +
We'll give you a real timeline for configuring or sourcing it. Our pre-repaired stock covers most common configurations.
Our unit is custom or modified. Can you handle it? +
Yes. In-house engineering and CAD let us work with non-standard configurations, and factory-trained technicians can adapt a standard gearbox to match your custom requirements.
What warranty comes with it? +
Every refurbished gearbox carries a full workmanship guarantee, with warranty coverage backed by factory-trained technical support.
